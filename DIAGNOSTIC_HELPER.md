# Diagnostic Helper Interface

A specialized healthcare AI agent designed for symptom analysis, differential diagnosis, and diagnostic test recommendations.

## 🎨 Design Features

### Visual Design
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot-inspired interface** with diagnostic specialization
- **Diagnostic-focused branding** with medical icons and professional colors
- **Responsive design** optimized for clinical workflows
- **Smooth animations** and professional interactions

### Diagnostic Capabilities

#### 1. Symptom Analysis
- **AI-powered analysis** of patient symptoms and complaints
- **Pattern recognition** for symptom correlation
- **Severity assessment** and urgency evaluation
- **Symptom tracking** and progression monitoring

#### 2. Test Recommendations
- **Evidence-based diagnostic test** suggestions and protocols
- **Test prioritization** based on clinical presentation
- **Cost-effectiveness** analysis for test selection
- **Follow-up testing** recommendations

#### 3. Differential Diagnosis
- **Comprehensive differential diagnosis** generation and ranking
- **Probability scoring** for each potential diagnosis
- **Evidence-based reasoning** for diagnostic decisions
- **Risk stratification** and urgency assessment

#### 4. Urgency Assessment
- **Patient urgency evaluation** and triage recommendations
- **Critical condition** identification and alerts
- **Emergency protocol** suggestions
- **Specialist referral** recommendations
