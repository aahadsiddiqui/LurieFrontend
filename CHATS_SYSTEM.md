# Chats System

A comprehensive chat management system with three specialized interfaces: Recent Conversations, Saved Chats, and Team Discussions, following Microsoft 365 Copilot design consistency.

## 🎨 Design Features

### Visual Design
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot-inspired interface** with chat specialization
- **Consistent design language** across all chat interfaces
- **Responsive design** optimized for healthcare workflows
- **Smooth animations** and professional interactions

## 📱 Chat Interfaces

### 1. Recent Conversations (`/chats/recent`)

#### Features
- **Conversation history** with comprehensive filtering and sorting
- **Multiple conversation types** (Chat, Clinical, Research, Diagnostic, Team)
- **Smart search** across titles, messages, and context
- **Unread indicators** and priority management
- **Pin and star** functionality for important conversations

#### Conversation Types
- **Chat** - General healthcare AI conversations
- **Clinical** - Clinical Assistant interactions
- **Research** - Research Bot discussions
- **Diagnostic** - Diagnostic Helper sessions
- **Team** - Team discussion channels

#### Key Features
- **Search and filter** by conversation type
- **Sort options** (Recent, Unread, Starred)
- **Pin conversations** for quick access
- **Star favorites** for important discussions
- **Message count** and activity indicators
- **Context tags** for easy identification

### 2. Saved Chats (`/chats/saved`)

#### Features
- **Bookmarked conversations** with personal notes
- **Favorited chat management** and organization
- **Search functionality** across titles, messages, and notes
- **Pin and unpin** saved conversations
- **Remove from saved** functionality
- **Personal notes** for each saved chat

#### Saved Chat Features
- **Bookmark indicator** with blue bookmark icon
- **Personal notes** for context and reminders
- **Saved date tracking** for organization
- **Pin functionality** for priority saved chats
- **Remove from saved** with confirmation
- **Search across notes** and content

#### Organization
- **Sort by saved date** (default)
- **Sort by pinned** status
- **Sort by recent activity**
- **Filter by conversation type**
- **Search across all content**

### 3. Team Discussions (`/chats/team`)

#### Microsoft Teams-like Interface
- **Team-based organization** with multiple healthcare teams
- **Channel structure** within each team
- **Real-time messaging** with Teams-like functionality
- **Team management** and member coordination
- **Channel types** for different purposes

#### Team Structure
- **Cardiology Department** - Cardiology team discussions
- **Emergency Medicine** - Emergency department coordination
- **Research Team** - Medical research collaboration
- **Custom teams** for different departments

#### Channel Types
- **General** - General team discussions
- **Clinical** - Patient care coordination
- **Research** - Research and studies
- **Admin** - Administrative discussions
- **Casual** - Informal team communication

#### Team Features
- **Team status indicators** (Active/Inactive)
- **Member count** and activity tracking
- **Last activity** timestamps
- **Team search** and filtering
- **Join team** functionality

#### Channel Features
- **Channel-specific messaging** with context
- **Unread indicators** and notification management
- **Pin channels** for quick access
- **Message count** and activity tracking
- **Channel descriptions** and context
- **Type-specific icons** and colors

#### Messaging Features
- **Real-time messaging** interface
- **Message reactions** (Like, Reply, React)
- **File attachments** and media sharing
- **Message threading** and replies
- **Message editing** and history
- **Emoji reactions** and interactions
