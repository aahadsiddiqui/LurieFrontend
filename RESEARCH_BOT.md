# Research Bot Interface

A specialized healthcare AI agent designed for medical literature analysis, research data interpretation, and evidence-based insights generation.

## 🎨 Design Features

### Visual Design
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot-inspired interface** with research specialization
- **Research-focused branding** with academic icons and professional colors
- **Responsive design** optimized for research workflows
- **Smooth animations** and professional interactions

### Research Capabilities

#### 1. Literature Search
- **Comprehensive search** across medical databases and research papers
- **Multi-database integration** (PubMed, Cochrane, etc.)
- **Advanced filtering** by publication year, impact factor, citations
- **Real-time results** with relevance scoring

#### 2. Data Analysis
- **Statistical analysis** and interpretation of research data
- **Meta-analysis** generation and systematic reviews
- **Trend analysis** across research domains
- **Visualization** of research findings

#### 3. Systematic Reviews
- **Automated systematic review** generation
- **Meta-analysis** of multiple studies
- **Evidence synthesis** and quality assessment
- **PRISMA compliance** for review standards

#### 4. Research Insights
- **AI-powered insights** and trend analysis
- **Research gap identification** and opportunities
- **Citation analysis** and impact assessment
- **Collaboration recommendations** based on research interests
