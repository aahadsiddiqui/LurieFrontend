# Clinical Assistant Interface

A specialized healthcare AI agent designed for clinical decision support, diagnostic assistance, and patient care optimization.

## 🎨 Design Features

### Visual Design
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot-inspired interface** with healthcare specialization
- **Clinical-focused branding** with medical icons and professional colors
- **Responsive design** optimized for healthcare workflows
- **Smooth animations** and professional interactions

### Clinical Capabilities

#### 1. Diagnostic Support
- **AI-powered diagnostic assistance** based on symptoms and medical history
- **Evidence-based recommendations** from clinical guidelines
- **Risk assessment** with confidence scoring
- **Differential diagnosis** suggestions

#### 2. Patient Monitoring
- **Continuous monitoring** of patient vitals and health indicators
- **Alert systems** for critical values
- **Trend analysis** for patient progress
- **Predictive insights** for patient outcomes

#### 3. Risk Assessment
- **Comprehensive risk analysis** for treatment planning
- **Patient safety** evaluation
- **Medication interaction** checking
- **Contraindication** warnings

#### 4. Treatment Optimization
- **Evidence-based treatment** recommendations
- **Protocol optimization** suggestions
- **Drug interaction** analysis
- **Dosage adjustment** recommendations
