# Documents AI System

A comprehensive document management system with AI-powered capabilities for document summarization, analysis, and insights, following Microsoft 365 Copilot design consistency.

## 🎨 Design Features

### Visual Design
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot-inspired interface** with AI integration
- **Consistent design language** across all document interfaces
- **Responsive design** optimized for healthcare workflows
- **Smooth animations** and professional interactions
- **AI-powered insights** and document analysis

## 📄 Documents Interface (`/documents`)

### Features
- **Document management** with comprehensive filtering and sorting
- **Multiple document types** (Documents, Spreadsheets, Presentations, Forms, Notebooks, Templates)
- **Smart search** across titles, descriptions, and tags
- **Category organization** (Clinical, Research, Emergency, Admin)
- **AI-powered document analysis** and summarization
- **Collaboration features** with team members
- **Pin and star** functionality for important documents

### AI Integration Capabilities

#### **AI Document Analysis**
- **Automatic summarization** of document content
- **Key insights extraction** from documents
- **Keyword identification** and tagging
- **Sentiment analysis** of document content
- **Confidence scoring** for AI analysis
- **Real-time AI assistance** for document understanding

#### **AI Features**
- **Document Summarization** - AI-generated summaries of document content
- **Content Analysis** - Deep analysis of document structure and content
- **Insight Generation** - Key insights and recommendations from documents
- **Keyword Extraction** - Automatic identification of important terms

- **Confidence Scoring** - AI confidence levels for analysis accuracy
